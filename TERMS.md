## The short version
Punchie is a one-time purchase app. No subscription, no ads. Use it for your own cards, tags, stickers and ornaments.

## Free and paid
Punchie is free to start. The Keepsake Pass, the Christmas Ornament Pack and Everything Forever are one-time purchases. You pay once and keep them. They are never renewed and never charged again.

## Refunds
Apple and Google handle payment and refunds. To ask for a refund, use the store you bought from. We cannot refund a purchase ourselves.

## Restoring
Tap Restore purchases in Settings to get your purchases back on a new phone or after reinstalling. Use the same Apple or Google account.

## Your photos and your work
Your photos and cutouts belong to you. Only use photos you have the right to use. We do not look at them and we do not keep them.

## What the prints are
The stamp-style edges and edition numbers are decoration. Printed pieces are not valid postage, not official stamps and not documents of any kind.

## Print quality
Punchie prepares sheets at true size. Your printer, your paper and your cutting decide how they come out. We do not promise lab quality, and we are not responsible for printer or paper results.

## Using the app
Punchie is provided as it is. We work to keep it reliable but cannot promise it will never have a fault. Our liability is limited to the price you paid, as far as the law allows.

## Changes
If these terms change, the new version is in the app with a new date.
