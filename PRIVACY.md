## The short version
Punchie collects nothing. Your photos, your cutouts and your settings stay on your phone.

## What stays on your phone
Your photos are read only to make your cutouts. The cutouts, your tree and your settings are saved on this phone and nowhere else. Nothing is uploaded. Punchie has no account and no sign-in.

## What we do not use
No analytics. No ads. No trackers. No crash reporting that leaves your phone. Punchie makes no network calls of its own.

## Permissions
Photos: only to let you choose a picture to cut, and, if you tap Save to Photos, to save a finished piece. Camera: only if you choose to take a new photo. You can say no to either and keep using Punchie with the other.

## Purchases
Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. They tell the app that a purchase is valid. We never see your name, your card or your account.

## Printing and sharing
When you print, save a PDF or share a card, your phone hands the file to the app or printer you choose. That is your choice each time, and it is outside Punchie.

## Children
Punchie does not collect anything from anyone, including children.

## Deleting your data
Delete a cutout inside the app, or delete the app, and everything Punchie saved is gone from your phone.
